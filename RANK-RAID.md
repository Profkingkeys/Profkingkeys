# Rank Raid: a 3D startup leaderboard on Mars

**Your startup's next opponent could be one brick above you.**

[Play Rank Raid at rankraid.world](https://rankraid.world/)

Rank Raid is a browser game for founders, startups, developers, businesses and creators who want to put their work on a public leaderboard. Your website or social profile becomes a brick in a 3D Mars landscape. Add your link, strengthen a brick, or drop a bomb to challenge a higher position.

## What you can do

- Explore global and category leaderboards.
- Add a website, product URL or social profile through a paid action.
- Raid a higher position with an amount greater than the target brick's strength.
- Fortify an existing brick or upgrade its rank.
- Open a company's website and inspect its outbound-click statistics.
- Browse the Mars scene on mobile, with dust, a day/night cycle and optional sound.

Browsing does not require signup. Paid actions use Flutterwave checkout and are spent on the selected action, rather than stored in a browser wallet. The entry price starts at US$0.10 equivalent; available currencies, payment methods and the applicable minimum are shown during checkout.

Company visit statistics measure clicks on **Visit site**, rather than confirmed sales or visits to the destination website. Ranking is part of the game, not an endorsement or a measure of product quality.

## Built for more than one kind of project

Bring a developer tool, AI product, agency, online store, education project, health and wellness business, creator tool, game or personal profile. Find a relevant category and see which projects occupy its tower.

**Founders: bring your product. Developers: bring your latest build. Creators: bring your profile.**
