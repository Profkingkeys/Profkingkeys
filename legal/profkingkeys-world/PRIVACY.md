# Profkingkeys World — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026. Version: 1.0.

Kingsley Umoh (Profkingkeys / PharmWeb3), Nigeria, operates Profkingkeys World.
For privacy requests, contact the operator through
[the PharmWeb3 portfolio](https://pharmweb3.com/portfolio). Do not send passwords or
tokens, or put personal account details in public GitHub issues.

## Information used

- **Guest access:** online guests receive a pseudonymous backend identity and session.
  A guest identity is not a claim of complete anonymity. Appearance and settings are
  saved on the device; the service uses district membership and temporary in-world
  movement, facing, seating and gesture data to show connected visitors.
- **Accounts:** full name, email, authentication records and an optional sign-in
  username are used for signup, login, verification, recovery and protected actions.
  Supabase Auth handles password authentication. Account appearance may be stored
  for restoration across sessions; session credentials are held in platform storage.
- **Business content:** business profiles, merchant contact details you choose to
  publish, catalogue entries, uploaded photos, adverts, ownership and term records,
  and moderation decisions support advertising and business discovery.
- **Activity and support:** visits, catalogue interactions, enquiry/composer activity,
  reports and audit records support metrics, moderation, security and troubleshooting.
  Opening an email composer is an activity event, not proof that an email was sent.
  Login abuse controls use a server-derived hash of network identity; infrastructure
  providers may also process IP addresses and request logs.

The app does not need your real-world precise location to show your avatar in the
virtual world. The Android photo picker provides photos you select; it does not
authorize unrestricted access to your photo library. Uploaded images are processed
into display derivatives; the image transformer strips embedded image metadata.
Raw uploads are private pending processing/review. Public derivatives and business
content are visible when published, and may be copied by other people.

## Purposes and recipients

Information is used to provide the service, restore preferences, manage accounts,
enforce business permissions and terms, moderate content, prevent abuse and measure
the activity the world actually observes. The operator relies on applicable lawful
bases for these purposes, including providing requested services, consent for optional
public contact/content choices, legitimate security interests and legal obligations.
Optional publication consent can be withdrawn through available owner controls or
by contacting the operator.

Supabase provides authentication, database, storage, functions and Realtime services.
GitHub hosts these policy pages and development resources. Their services may process
data outside Nigeria under their applicable arrangements. The operator must apply
the safeguards required by applicable law to transfers. These pages do not claim
that all data is stored in Nigeria. Merchant enquiries opened in your email app are
also handled by your email provider and the merchant you contact.

Other visitors receive an in-world peer identifier and the appearance/movement needed
to display your avatar. Your email, password, authentication token and real-world
location are not part of the public movement payload. Public merchant contacts and
adverts are separate from private authentication records. No in-world player text
or voice chat is provided in this version.

## Retention and security

Account, business, moderation, entitlement and audit records are kept as needed for
the service, lawful obligations and dispute handling. A universal automatic deletion
period is not currently configured; do not assume signing out or a term expiring
erases every related record. Temporary district membership expires unless renewed.
Device preferences remain until changed or the app's data is removed. Copies of public
content held by recipients or external providers may remain under their own policies.

Access controls, private storage and protected session handling reduce risk; no system
can guarantee absolute security. Keep your account and device protected. The operator
will handle incidents and any required notifications under applicable law.

## Your choices and rights

You may explore as a guest, change device settings/appearance, sign out, choose what
business contact details to publish, and use available reporting and content controls.
Contact the operator privately to request access, correction, export, deletion,
restriction or to object or withdraw applicable consent. Identity verification may
be needed; legal retention requirements may limit deletion of particular records.
Logging out is not account deletion. You may raise a complaint with the Nigeria Data
Protection Commission or another competent regulator under applicable law.

Parents/guardians should supervise users who cannot lawfully consent or agree to the
service. Do not upload children's personal information; contact the operator if such
information needs removal. Material policy changes will carry an updated version/date.
