## What the evidence does not establish

A successful software build does not prove clinical safety, learning effectiveness, trading profitability, enterprise scale or research novelty. The portfolio links the implementation and its limitations so collaborators can judge it directly.

## A useful review path

Download a game and complete a correct and incorrect route. Read its domain tests. Run the data-analysis lab and reproduce 80.75%. Inspect the Android workflow and its APK artifact. Then suggest one concrete improvement with a reproducible example.
