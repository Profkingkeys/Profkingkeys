# Leadership Principles

## Mission

Use advanced artificial intelligence and product engineering to close important gaps in healthcare, education, safety, and access.

## Operating principles

1. **Problem before platform.** Begin with a person, decision, and measurable cost of failure.
2. **Evidence before confidence.** Fluent output is not proof. Instrument the system and preserve the path to evidence.
3. **Authority stays explicit.** A model can propose. Identity, policy, deterministic code, and qualified humans authorize.
4. **Prototype honestly.** Ambition belongs in the vision. Maturity labels belong in the repository.
5. **Design for review.** Architecture, tests, documentation, and failure modes should be inspectable by another engineer or domain expert.
6. **Protect the vulnerable user.** Children, patients, and learners are not engagement metrics.
7. **Build reusable capability.** Extract domain engines, evaluation methods, and safety patterns that strengthen future products.

## Leadership evidence

Leadership in this portfolio is demonstrated through program charters, contribution paths, review standards, safety boundaries, technical roadmaps, and working foundations that other people can inspect and improve.
